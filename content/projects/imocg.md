## A platform for the competition

I built a ticketing platform for the International Martini Organ Competition Groningen between April and August 2024.

The platform supports multiple events and tickets that cover more than one event, with a shopping cart, optional donations, and Mollie payments.

## Behind the scenes

An organizer dashboard brings together orders, email delivery, and attendance. A ticket scanner keeps track of which tickets have been used.
